## Implementation summary (high level)

- **Authorship metadata.** The blog content collection schema now accepts two
  optional fields: `authorship` (enum: `human`, `human-ai-assisted`,
  `ai-generated`) and `withdrawn` (boolean). Both are intentionally optional;
  no existing post has had frontmatter changed under `unknown`.
- **Central rendering.** Withdrawal behavior is implemented in the post
  template (`src/pages/[...slug].astro`), not as ad hoc source rewrites. The
  notice is a semantic `<aside>` styled prominently; the article's original
  prose is wrapped in a container with a readable strikethrough treatment;
  the byline, footer, navigation, and site chrome are not struck through.
- **Permanent authorship statement.** Added at `/about` (new page) —
  `src/pages/about.astro` — using the site's existing visual language. Brief,
  humane, non-defensive; covers Eric's use of AI and his disclosure standard.
- **Confessional Booth draft.** Created at
  `src/content/blog/2026/08/confessional-booth.md` with a date of 2026-08-26,
  permalink `/2026/08/26/confessional-booth.html`, frontmatter
  `authorship: human`, and a blank body. All withdrawal notices link to this
  URL.
- **No destructive changes.** No posts deleted, no slugs/permalinks/dates
  changed, no canonical URLs altered, no redirects added, no provenance
  database, no AI detection, no badges on normal posts.
